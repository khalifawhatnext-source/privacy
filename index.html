Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 17, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to VTU Connect ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application to purchase mobile data, airtime, and other utilities.

2. Information We Collect

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when registering on the application or processing a transaction. This may include:

  • Personal Details: Name, email address, and phone number.
  • Payment Information: We may collect data necessary to process your payments, such as your payment instrument number (e.g., credit card number) and the security code associated with your payment instrument. Note: All payment data is stored by our payment processors and we do not store full card details on our servers.
  • Transaction Data: Details of your airtime, data, and bill payment purchases.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect or receive to:

  • Facilitate account creation and logon process.
  • Process your VTU transactions (airtime, data, cable TV, electricity) swiftly and securely.
  • Send you administrative information, such as transaction receipts and policy updates.
  • Improve our app's user experience and provide targeted customer support.

4. Data Security

We have implemented appropriate technical and organizational security measures designed to protect the security of any personal information we process. Our application utilizes industry-standard encryption protocols to ensure your transaction data remains strictly confidential and protected from unauthorized access.

5. Third-Party Services

We may share your data with third-party vendors, service providers, contractors, or agents who perform services for us or on our behalf and require access to such information to do that work. This includes payment processing gateways and telecommunication network providers necessary for fulfilling your VTU orders.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy notice from time to time. The updated version will be indicated by an updated "Last Updated" date and the updated version will be effective as soon as it is accessible. We encourage you to review this privacy notice frequently to be informed of how we are protecting your information.
